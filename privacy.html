Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how HowFlow Uploader (“the app”) handles data. The app is a private command-line tool operated by the owner of the HowFlow YouTube channel. Its only purpose is to upload HowFlow's own videos and their metadata to the HowFlow channel.

Who can use the app

Only the HowFlow channel owner. The app is not distributed or offered to the public, and it never accesses any other person's Google or YouTube account.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedules to the HowFlow channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) — to confirm which channel is connected (channel name, subscriber and video counts).

The app does not read comments, viewer data, messages, email, contacts, or any other Google data.

How data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the channel owner's own computer. They are never uploaded to a server, sold, or shared with any third party. Video files and metadata are sent only to YouTube.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell, or transfer Google user data to anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access & deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or via Google's security settings. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data held by the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the HowFlow YouTube channel's About page.
